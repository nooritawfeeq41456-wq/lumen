    HOW TO CONTROL

    HAND SIGNS

    ✋ Open hand
    Move it to spin the hologram
    ✌ Peace (hold)
    Shape mode on / off. In shape mode, sweep your hand left or right and the shapes slide in one after another: brain → globe → solar system → heart → chart
    👆 Air click
    Point at anything (a button, a planet, a brain part), then quickly pinch: it clicks. Or keep pointing for a second
    🤏 Pinch (hold)
    Grab and turn the hologram precisely
    🙌 Two hands
    Apart to zoom in, together to zoom out · pinch with both and pull apart to explode the brain
    👍 Thumbs up
    Open the swipe menu (swipe to browse, pinch to choose) · answers “yes”
    👎 Thumbs down
    No / cancel / back
    🫸 Push
    Push your open palm toward the camera: back / close
    🤘 Rock sign
    Turn listening on or off (then just say “Lumen …”)
    ✊ Fist
    Freeze · leaves shape mode · goes back in the swipe menu
    ✌ moving
    With a panel open, move a peace sign up or down to scroll it

    Signs not working well? Open Menu → Train signs and teach LUMEN your own hand. Good light and an arm’s length from the camera help a lot.

        MAKE LUMEN SMARTER

        The built-in brain runs on your laptop, so it is small. A free Google Gemini key gives LUMEN a much stronger brain that understands you better and can see through the camera. It costs nothing.

        1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
        2. Press Create API key, then copy the key.
        3. Paste it below and press Connect.

        Your key stays only in this browser. The free tier has daily limits, and Google may use free-tier chats to improve its products, so don’t share private things. If you are offline, LUMEN switches back to the built-in brain by itself.

        STOP THE PERMISSION POP-UPS

        Chrome never remembers “Allow” for a page opened as a plain file, so it keeps asking. Open LUMEN from a real web address and it asks only once, ever.

        First: right now LUMEN is running from inside the zip. Right-click the zip file → Extract All, and use the extracted folder.

        BEST: YOUR OWN FREE LUMEN WEBSITE (GITHUB PAGES)

        1. Go to github.com and make a free account.
        2. Press + (top right) → New repository. Name it lumen, keep it Public, tick Add a README file, press Create repository.
        3. Press Add file → Upload files, drag in index.html, LUMEN.html and Jarvis.html, then press Commit changes.
        4. Open Settings → Pages. Under “Branch” pick main and /(root), press Save.
        5. Wait 1–2 minutes. Your LUMEN is at https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/lumen/. Bookmark it, open it, and press Allow once.

        Anyone who knows the link could open the page, but your memory, keys and data stay only in your own browser. To update LUMEN later, upload the new files the same way.
